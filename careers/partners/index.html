Who we collaborate with

Different strengths. Shared ambition.

We are open to conversations with organisations that can help people access practical experience—and with businesses that can contribute meaningful projects or opportunities.

01

Universities & colleges

Build a bridge between campus communities and practical professional exposure through student outreach, project pathways and structured collaborations.

02

Training & coaching institutes

Complement skills training with practical assignments and experience-led opportunities that help learners demonstrate what they can do.

03

Recruitment & placement partners

Collaborate on early-career readiness, candidate development and pathways that help job seekers strengthen their professional profiles.

04

Businesses & employers

Bring forward suitable business projects, briefs or professional challenges that can be scoped into supervised, outcome-focused work.

05

Professional communities

Work with associations, networks and communities to reach relevant audiences and create useful learning or career-development initiatives.

06

Channel & referral partners

Explore audience-sharing, referral or co-marketing arrangements where there is a clear mutual benefit and a transparent process.

A clear starting point

How a partnership takes shape.

We start with fit and clarity—not a one-size-fits-all package.

01

Understand the fit

We learn about your organisation, the people you serve and what you would like the partnership to achieve.

02

Shape the model

We map the audience, responsibilities, delivery approach, timelines and—where relevant—commercial or revenue-sharing terms.

03

Start with a pilot

Where appropriate, we recommend a focused first initiative, review what works and agree how to scale it.
